Screen: the single-player song-quiz answering screen of a Japanese idol music game called "SHINY COLORS SONG GUESS". The player hears a 6-second instrumental (no vocals) and must identify the song from four choices.

VISUAL WORLD (identical across all comps, non-negotiable):
Design language "Song for Prism". Bright, airy, cool-lavender idol aesthetic. NOT dark mode.
- Ground: near-white #f7f6fb washed with an extremely faint holographic/iridescent laser-film sheen (pale pink, mint, cyan, butter yellow) plus a barely visible crystal-shard polygon texture at rgba(97,95,144,0.03).
- Content surfaces: translucent white glass, rgba(255,255,255,0.6), with backdrop blur, so the iridescence shows faintly through.
- The ONLY brand color is indigo-violet #615f90. The only bright accent is cyan #5ee2ff, with deep sky #00b4f0. Pale lavender #a2a2c0 for dividers. Soft pink #ffbad6 used sparingly.
- A thin prism rainbow hairline (magenta -> cyan -> yellow -> coral) is the one rainbow element.
- Shadows are PURPLE drop-shadows rgba(71,68,150,0.25). Never black shadows. No glow.
- Border-radius is essentially ZERO. All shape comes from clip-path diagonal cuts: parallelograms with a sliced corner for buttons/labels/nav bars, rectangles with top-left AND bottom-right corners sliced for content cards, hexagons for avatars.
- Typography: Noto Sans JP for Japanese text, Jost geometric sans for uppercase Latin. Very wide letter-spacing (0.1em body, 0.2em Latin caps). Only weights 400/600/700. A small katakana label sits directly above each large uppercase Latin heading, flanked by violet angular corner brackets.
- Text is high contrast: body copy is near-black on white, violet is used for headings and structure, never for small body text.
Render as a crisp flat UI design mockup / product screenshot, no browser chrome, no device frame, no photograph, no 3D. Landscape desktop web viewport.

STRUCTURE — "THE RUNNING LIGHT":
The entire screen has exactly ONE moving element: a single unbroken horizontal light band spanning the full viewport width, sitting in the upper third. It is the countdown, the audio spectrum, and the progress bar all at once.
- The band is a thin prism-rainbow strip roughly 8px tall at its base, currently retracted from both ends toward the center to show about 55% time remaining; the retracted portions are pale lavender #a2a2c0 at 20% opacity.
- Growing directly out of the top edge of that band is a live audio spectrum: ~70 thin vertical bars of varying height (2px to 40px), each colored by its position along the prism gradient, tightly packed, reacting to music.
- Immediately above the band, left-aligned: a small katakana label "リスニング" in violet with wide letter-spacing, and beneath it a large uppercase Jost heading "LISTENING" in #615f90, flanked by violet angular corner brackets.
- On the right end above the band: a monospace counter "07 / 20" and score "1240" in violet, tabular figures, wide tracking.
- Below the band, a single left-aligned column of FOUR answer bars stacked vertically with generous gaps. Each bar is a translucent white glass parallelogram (left edge sliced diagonally), full width, about 76px tall, with a purple drop-shadow. Each has: a narrow vertical color cap at its sliced left edge in a unit color; a small violet keycap numeral 1/2/3/4; then the Japanese song title in bold Noto Sans JP near-black at 17px, with the artist name below in gray at 12px.
- The four song titles, exactly: "アルストロメリア", "シャイノグラフィ", "無自覚アプリオリ", "バベルシティ・グレイス". Artists below: "アルストロメリア", "放課後クライマックスガールズ", "アンティーカ", "ストレイライト".
- The left color caps are, in order: pink #ff699e, orange #fa8333, purple #853998, crimson #af011c.
- Bottom-left, a small ghost parallelogram button "↻ 重听 (1)" in violet outline.
- Everything except the light band is perfectly still and quiet. Huge amounts of white space. No cards around the whole page, no header bar, no sidebar, no footer.
