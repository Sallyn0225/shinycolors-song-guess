Screen: the single-player song-quiz answering screen of a Japanese idol music game called "SHINY COLORS SONG GUESS". The player hears a 6-second instrumental and picks the song from four choices.

VISUAL WORLD (identical across all comps, non-negotiable):
Design language "Song for Prism". Bright, airy, cool-lavender idol aesthetic. NOT dark mode.
- Ground: near-white #f7f6fb washed with an extremely faint holographic/iridescent laser-film sheen (pale pink, mint, cyan, butter yellow) plus a barely visible crystal-shard polygon texture at rgba(97,95,144,0.03).
- Content surfaces: translucent white glass, rgba(255,255,255,0.6), with backdrop blur, so the iridescence shows faintly through.
- The ONLY brand color is indigo-violet #615f90. The only bright accent is cyan #5ee2ff, with deep sky #00b4f0. Pale lavender #a2a2c0 for dividers. Soft pink #ffbad6 used sparingly.
- A thin prism rainbow hairline (magenta -> cyan -> yellow -> coral) is the one rainbow element.
- Shadows are PURPLE drop-shadows rgba(71,68,150,0.25). Never black shadows. No glow.
- Border-radius is essentially ZERO. All shape comes from clip-path diagonal cuts: parallelograms with a sliced corner for buttons/labels/nav bars, rectangles with top-left AND bottom-right corners sliced for content cards, hexagons for avatars.
- Typography: Noto Sans JP for Japanese text, Jost geometric sans for uppercase Latin. Very wide letter-spacing (0.1em body, 0.2em Latin caps). Only weights 400/600/700. A small katakana label sits directly above each large uppercase Latin heading, flanked by violet angular corner brackets.
- Text is high contrast: body copy is near-black on white, violet is used for headings and structure, never for small body text.
Render as a crisp flat UI design mockup / product screenshot, no browser chrome, no device frame, no photograph, no 3D. Landscape desktop web viewport.

STRUCTURE — "THE STEP ROW", the grammar of an early-80s drum machine translated into this white prism world (take the GRAMMAR, never the charcoal panel or LED glow — the ground stays white and iridescent):
- Dominating the upper half, a single unbroken horizontal row of TWENTY step keys, one per question, spanning the full viewport width, never wrapping. Each key is a small slanted parallelogram about 56px wide and 56px tall with a purple drop-shadow, carrying a tiny violet numeral 01-20 silkscreened in tracked Jost caps at its base.
- Keys 01-04 are lit solid cyan #5ee2ff (answered correctly). Keys 05-06 are pale flat gray (missed). Key 07 is the CHASE LIGHT position: outlined thick in violet #615f90 and washed with a bright prism gradient, visibly the "now". Keys 08-20 are empty faint violet outlines on white — an invitation.
- Directly beneath the step row, a thin full-width prism-rainbow hairline acting as the transport clock, with a small triangular playhead marker sitting exactly under key 07.
- Top-left above the row: small katakana "ステップ" over uppercase Jost "PATTERN 07 / 20" in violet with angular corner brackets. Top-right: a segmented readout showing "04" seconds in large violet monospace with tiny "SEC" beneath, and "1240" score.
- Lower half: four answer keys laid out as one horizontal row of four equal slanted parallelogram pads, each about 300px wide, translucent white glass, purple drop-shadow, with a solid unit-color chip along the top edge, a big violet numeral 1-4 in the corner, the Japanese song title in bold Noto Sans JP near-black 17px centered, and the artist in gray 12px beneath.
- The four song titles, exactly: "アルストロメリア", "シャイノグラフィ", "無自覚アプリオリ", "バベルシティ・グレイス". Artists: "アルストロメリア", "放課後クライマックスガールズ", "アンティーカ", "ストレイライト". Top chips: pink #ff699e, orange #fa8333, purple #853998, crimson #af011c.
- Machine-like precision, everything on one strict rhythmic grid, but rendered in bright white glass and violet, not in dark plastic.
