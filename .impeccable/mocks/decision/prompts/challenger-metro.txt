Screen: the single-player song-quiz answering screen of a Japanese idol music game called "SHINY COLORS SONG GUESS". The player hears a 6-second instrumental and picks the song from four choices.

VISUAL WORLD (identical across all comps, non-negotiable):
Design language "Song for Prism". Bright, airy, cool-lavender idol aesthetic. NOT dark mode.
- Ground: near-white #f7f6fb washed with an extremely faint holographic/iridescent laser-film sheen (pale pink, mint, cyan, butter yellow) plus a barely visible crystal-shard polygon texture at rgba(97,95,144,0.03).
- Content surfaces: translucent white glass, rgba(255,255,255,0.6), with backdrop blur, so the iridescence shows faintly through.
- The ONLY brand color is indigo-violet #615f90. The only bright accent is cyan #5ee2ff, with deep sky #00b4f0. Pale lavender #a2a2c0 for dividers. Soft pink #ffbad6 used sparingly.
- A thin prism rainbow hairline (magenta -> cyan -> yellow -> coral) is the one rainbow element.
- Shadows are PURPLE drop-shadows rgba(71,68,150,0.25). Never black shadows. No glow.
- Border-radius is essentially ZERO. All shape comes from clip-path diagonal cuts: parallelograms with a sliced corner for buttons/labels/nav bars, rectangles with top-left AND bottom-right corners sliced for content cards, hexagons for avatars.
- Typography: Noto Sans JP for Japanese text, Jost geometric sans for uppercase Latin. Very wide letter-spacing (0.1em body, 0.2em Latin caps). Only weights 400/600/700. A small katakana label sits directly above each large uppercase Latin heading, flanked by violet angular corner brackets.
- Text is high contrast: body copy is near-black on white, violet is used for headings and structure, never for small body text.
Render as a crisp flat UI design mockup / product screenshot, no browser chrome, no device frame, no photograph, no 3D. Landscape desktop web viewport.

STRUCTURE — "TYPE IS THE INTERFACE" (Metro's typographic grammar translated into this white prism world — take the GRAMMAR only; the ground stays white and iridescent, never black, and the accents stay violet and cyan):
- There is almost no chrome. No borders on the answer areas, no shadows on them, no buttons. The song titles ARE the interface, set enormous.
- Upper-left: a giant lightweight Jost heading "LISTENING" set very large, its right edge cropped hard off the viewport edge mid-letter, in pale violet #a2a2c0. Above it a tiny tracked katakana "リスニング" in solid violet.
- Upper-right: a huge violet countdown numeral "04" set at poster scale, with tiny tracked "SECONDS" beneath, and a thin prism-rainbow hairline running under it as the only decoration.
- The lower two-thirds: four Japanese song titles set as enormous type, stacked left-aligned as four lines, each at a different optical weight to express hierarchy. Each title's FIRST TWO OR THREE CHARACTERS are set in heavy weight solid near-black at full size (this is the 決まり字, the identifying head of the title), and the REMAINDER of each title continues in light weight pale gray at the same size. This weight break inside the word is the key mechanic and must be clearly visible.
- The four song titles, exactly, in this order and with the heavy/light break after the third character: "アルストロメリア", "シャイノグラフィ", "無自覚アプリオリ", "バベルシティ・グレイス".
- To the left of each title, a small tracked violet numeral 1-4 and a short solid unit-color bar: pink #ff699e, orange #fa8333, purple #853998, crimson #af011c.
- The second title is the hovered state: the entire line's area floods edge-to-edge with a flat cyan #5ee2ff field, and its type reverses to white.
- Beneath each title, in tiny tracked gray caps, the artist: "アルストロメリア", "放課後クライマックスガールズ", "アンティーカ", "ストレイライト".
- Bottom-left corner: tiny tracked violet "07 / 20 · SCORE 1240 · REPLAY 1".
- Extremely typographic, generous white space, no containers at all, type running to and past the edges.
